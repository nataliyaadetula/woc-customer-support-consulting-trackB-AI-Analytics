# WOC Customer Support Consulting Project

## Project track

**Business intelligence, CRM, decision support systems, and responsible AI**

This repository contains consulting artifacts for WOC's customer-support improvement project. The work evaluates customer interactions across phone, email, web forms, and social media to identify where analytics, a centralized CRM, and human-supervised AI can improve response time, consistency, and customer satisfaction.

## WOC problem statement

WOC's support channels operate independently, leaving customer data fragmented and limiting managers' visibility into support performance. Customers may receive inconsistent answers, staff repeatedly handle routine questions, and trends across topics and channels are difficult to see. The recommended direction is a centralized CRM supported by business-intelligence dashboards and responsible AI assistance. AI should help summarize, classify, and draft routine responses while employees retain responsibility for complex, sensitive, inaccurate, or low-confidence cases.

## Suggested final recommendation

WOC should phase in a unified CRM that consolidates interaction history, feeds dashboards for response time and topic/channel trends, and uses AI as a staff copilot for routing, summarization, knowledge retrieval, and draft responses. Human review should be required for escalations, personal-data requests, safety-sensitive questions, policy exceptions, and low-confidence AI output. Leadership should monitor response time, first-contact resolution, satisfaction, repeat contacts, employee overrides, and AI error/escation rates.
